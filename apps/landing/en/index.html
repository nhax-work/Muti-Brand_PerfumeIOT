Why ScentStation

A new sales channel, no store required.

Customers shouldn't have to buy a full bottle to find out whether a scent suits them. A paid spray right at the machine lets your fragrance be tried, remembered and sold.

  • Meet customers where they pass by

    Machines stand in public places. Customers see your brand name, scent family and price on screen, then try exactly your scent.

  • Set your price per spray

    You decide the price of each product, with no floor and no ceiling. Every slot selling the same product uses the same price.

  • Spray revenue is yours

    We collect payments on your behalf and pay out monthly. Payment gateway fees and customer refunds are on us.

  • No operations to manage

    Ship your bottles to our warehouse. We install them, track the remaining volume, restock and maintain the machines.

  • Your stock is protected

    Every slot comes with a storage plan. Bottles damaged while in our care are compensated at the plan's rate, up to its limit.

  • Data only you can see

    Revenue and spray reports for every slot. Other brands on the same machine never know you are there.

Frequently asked questions

FAQ for brands

What does it cost?

Each slot needs a prepaid 3, 6 or 12-month plan and one storage plan. We take no percentage of your spray revenue.

When do I receive my revenue?

Spray revenue accumulates over the month, is settled after the month ends and then transferred to you. Before the first payout, you provide your tax code, business licence and payout account for us to verify. If the figures look wrong, you can raise a dispute before the money is sent.

Who supplies and restocks the bottles?

You ship bottles to our warehouse with proof that they are genuine. Our warehouse staff install them in your slot. When a slot runs low, the system reminds you to send a restock request.

Can other brands on the same machine see my data?

No. Each brand only sees its own slots, products and revenue; other brands' slots simply show as unavailable.

What if a customer pays and the machine fails?

The screen shows an incident QR code so the customer can request a refund. Our operations team handles it and we cover the refund.

Can I cancel a paid plan?

Paid plans cannot be cancelled or refunded. If no bottle has been installed yet, you can switch once to another free slot at the same price within the allowed window.

What if I don't want to renew?

Tell us online before the expiry date that you won't renew and collect your stock, with no grace fee. If you neither tell us nor renew, the slot enters a paid grace period; after that, any remaining stock is liquidated.
